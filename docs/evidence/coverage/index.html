Later productivity estimates face participation and timing bias

METR reports that later study participation, task selection, and concurrent-agent time measurement make its updated productivity estimate unreliable.

Limits: The authors consider greater speedup plausible but say their data weakly identifies its size. This is not a clean reversal of the earlier randomized result.

EVID-160 · No term relationship recorded

Application activity increases do not themselves measure useful output

Source-reported finding: In telemetry from eleven companies, Yu and colleagues estimate that frequent Copilot users increased productivity-application actions by 21.2% and communication actions by 7.1%. The measured units are application actions. Observation period and measurement context: January–September 2024; ten weeks before and twenty after enablement.

Limits: Activity is not output quality, time saved or productivity itself. Adoption selection remains. Reduced email activity may remove redundancy or reduce information exchange; these interpretations are not resolved by action counts.

EVID-189 · No term relationship recorded

Binance internal AI tooling in a reported multi-stage compromise

Binance reports that a compromised employee computer and an internal AI tool enabled further access and discovery of a container deployment token.

Limits: Initial issuer account; investigation ongoing. The internal AI mechanism is not disclosed sufficiently to establish prompt injection, autonomous attacker coordination, corruption of assurance information or a measured intervention-window contraction. The reported internal-AI-tool access path does not identify altered decision inputs or assurance records, so it does not establish a reference-environment-integrity failure.

How these counts work

Linked findings: distinct published evidence IDs with an explicit relationship to the term. Withdrawn records are excluded. Bibliography mentions and keyword matches alone do not count.

Recorded families: distinct recorded source-family identifiers across all sources cited by those linked findings. Multiple source versions in one family count once. Family labels do not certify independence, and recorded source dependencies can reveal further overlap. Missing family metadata is disclosed separately.

Qualifying and challenging: findings with those explicit relationship types. Other categories, including partial relationships, remain visible in the breakdown. Zero challenging links means none are recorded here; it does not establish consensus.

What to investigate next: check existing records for a defensible missing connection, seek directly public root sources, and retain contrary findings and limits. A gap does not justify forcing a finding into a term or adding a new term automatically.
