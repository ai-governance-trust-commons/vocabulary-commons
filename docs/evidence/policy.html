Publication and verification

The Evidence Library makes reported findings, counterevidence, and open questions available for examination alongside the Commons vocabulary.

Provisional publication

Records can be published before independent verification. A provisional label means the account remains open to examination, correction, qualification, or withdrawal. Publication does not endorse the source’s claims or establish that an event occurred as described.

Before routine publication, editorial checks cover attribution, source locations, case identity, source dependencies, stated uncertainty, and privacy. The founding steward has authorized AI-assisted preparation of provisional records without individual approval of each update. Material disputes, uncertain provenance, privacy or rights concerns, and proposed changes to the founding glossary are escalated for further review.

Three separate judgments

  • Publication status: whether a record is available in this collection.
  • Source inspection: whether the cited publication and supporting location have been examined.
  • Independent verification: whether underlying claims have been verified beyond the source’s account.

The initial collection is not independently verified by the Commons. Package checksums establish file integrity, not factual truth. Multiple publications derived from one study or event are not automatically independent corroboration.

Corrections and changes

Each record has a stable identifier and a correction link. Please identify the record, relevant source passage, proposed correction, and reason. You can also submit through the Commons participation routes, including the existing form without a GitHub account. Records and interpretations remain revisable; dated releases and change history preserve earlier versions. A correction should be visible, rather than silently replacing a materially different earlier account.

Sources and the founding glossary

The public collection contains citations and summaries, not archived copies of third-party publications. Preservation and redistribution require separate permission decisions. Links to the 35 founding terms do not amend their definitions or confer factual support on every application of those terms.

Evidence reporting categories

These categories describe the account held, separately from the glossary’s claim-standing scale. Reported observation identifies an attributed account and does not assert that the Commons demonstrated the event. Indication identifies a qualified inference. Hypothesis identifies an untested proposition. Conceptual framing and Normative proposal identify analysis and recommendations. Attributed author and issuer accounts, including controlled demonstrations, are labeled Reported observation in this release. The glossary’s Demonstrated (bounded) standing is a different classification and is not a verification label for a Library record. Detailed original assessments and limitations remain visible.

Prepared by identifies an AI assistant. POLICY-001 authorizes routine provisional publication; it does not signify individual human review of each record. Individual human review is shown as not recorded unless an explicit record establishes otherwise.

Dataset version identifies a content release; format_version identifies its schema. A null dispute-check date means no dated systematic check is recorded, not that disputes are absent.

Collection scope and intake

The Library collects information relevant to governance in human–AI environments, including oversight, organizational reliance, assurance, and risks arising from interacting activities. Eligibility does not require an incident, an adverse outcome, or a founding glossary match. Relevant unmatched evidence is deliberately retained.

A case is a grouping for a study, research series, incident, or operational setting; CASE identifiers are not incident counts. Counterevidence describes a relationship to a claim, not a credibility rating.

Measurement intake records the observation period, population, unit and denominator, method, uncertainty, and comparability limits where relevant. Missing details are stated as not reported or not established. Publication date is separate from the observation period; one snapshot does not establish a trend. Source-reported findings and Commons interpretations remain separate, and inspecting a publication does not independently verify its measurements.

Worked measurement example

The workplace Copilot study measures application actions across eleven companies during January–September 2024. Its reported 21.2% increase concerns productivity-application actions, not a measured 21.2% gain in useful output. Adoption selection and uncertain quality/time effects limit interpretation. Its August 2026 publication date is not the measurement period, and the observation is retained without forcing a glossary relationship.

Publication holds and corrections

Unresolved publication holds block routine approval and export. Dependent records must also be eligible. Withdrawals remain in private history; previously public identifiers retain a public notice without republishing the held claim.
