Publication and verification

The Evidence Locker makes reported findings, counterevidence, and open questions available for examination alongside the Commons vocabulary.

Provisional publication

Records can be published before independent verification. A provisional label means the account remains open to examination, correction, qualification, or withdrawal. Publication does not endorse the source’s claims or establish that an event occurred as described.

Before routine publication, editorial checks cover attribution, source locations, case identity, source dependencies, stated uncertainty, and privacy. The founding steward has authorized AI-assisted preparation of provisional records without individual approval of each update. Material disputes, uncertain provenance, privacy or rights concerns, and proposed changes to the founding glossary are escalated for further review.

Three separate judgments

  • Publication status: whether a record is available in this collection.
  • Source inspection: whether the cited publication and supporting location have been examined.
  • Independent verification: whether underlying claims have been verified beyond the source’s account.

The initial collection is not independently verified by the Commons. Package checksums establish file integrity, not factual truth. Multiple publications derived from one study or event are not automatically independent corroboration.

Corrections and changes

Each record has a stable identifier and a correction link. Please identify the record, relevant source passage, proposed correction, and reason. You can also submit through the Commons participation routes, including the existing form without a GitHub account. Records and interpretations remain revisable; dated releases and change history preserve earlier versions. A correction should be visible, rather than silently replacing a materially different earlier account.

Sources and the founding glossary

The public collection contains citations and summaries, not archived copies of third-party publications. Preservation and redistribution require separate permission decisions. Links to the 35 founding terms do not amend their definitions or confer factual support on every application of those terms.
