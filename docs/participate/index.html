Editorial review policy ↗

What happens after you submit?

Form submissions go to the founding steward for consideration. Public sharing follows the permission you select in the form. Providing an email address is optional; include one if you would like us to be able to follow up.

How the Commons develops

The founding reference is fixed; the Commons is not. The 35 published entries remain an unaltered, citable baseline. New terms, alternatives, evidence, and proposed revisions may be shared through GitHub Discussions and Issues (GitHub sign-in required) or submitted through the Tally form (no GitHub account required; email optional). Tally contributors choose whether their submission may be shared publicly. Accepted changes to a future curated edition will be traceable to public review and attributed decisions; they will not silently rewrite the founding publication.

During the initial phase, the founding steward coordinates editorial decisions. Additional independent reviewers will be sought for materially contested decisions when practical, with disagreements recorded rather than suppressed. Editorial decision rights and contributor roles will be documented as participation develops. Read the current editorial policy ↗

Principles worth preserving

Proposals and alternative definitions may remain publicly visible even if they are not incorporated into the curated glossary. Inclusion represents a current best fit, not an institutional guarantee.

Contributors and reviewers may work together to supply clarification, provenance, or supporting and contradictory evidence. New terminology should improve a meaningful distinction rather than needlessly replace established usage.
