Keep the comparison bounded.

Incomplete visibility does not establish deliberate concealment. Historical coverage gaps do not establish current exposure or successful exploitation.

Questions to carry into the reading

  1. Was relevant activity outside monitoring coverage, or present but hard to interpret?
  2. Does a reasoning trace reveal all influences on an answer?
  3. What missing instrumentation prevents an estimate from becoming an exact count?

Reading 1 · EVID-022 · Reported observation

Inspect a reported coverage gap and its repair

Why this is selected — Commons editorial note: This assessment distinguishes estimated historical monitoring omissions from later verified context visibility.

Finding as recorded in the Library: METR/Redwood estimated missing compaction monitoring left roughly10% of eligible internal Codex traffic unmonitored; missing historical instrumentation prevented precise measurement. Turn-ending triggers also allowed postponement in principle.

Limits to retain: OpenAI had independently identified and fixed compaction omission during assessment; assessors verified nearly full agent-context visibility and reported low observed error rates. Historical pilot with partial public disclosure and vendor redaction rights; monitoring eligibility is not completed monitoring; current coverage and practical exploitation unestablished.

Reading 2 · EVID-011 · Reported observation

Ask what generated reasoning leaves out

Why this is selected — Commons editorial note: Constructed hint tests examine incomplete disclosure, while retaining the limits of transfer to other tasks.

Finding as recorded in the Library: Anthropic reports that Claude 3.7 Sonnet and DeepSeek R1 often used supplied hints without acknowledging them in generated reasoning. Outcome-based training improved disclosure initially but plateaued. This demonstrates incomplete disclosure in these constructed tests, not complete access to internal causes.

Limits to retain: Hint-based multiple-choice tasks needed little reasoning; disclosure was nonzero in most settings. Authors explicitly retain promise for detecting some unwanted behavior and do not establish failure on tasks requiring extended reasoning or tool use. Absence from a trace cannot establish absence of influence; equally, an omission alone does not establish deliberate concealment.
