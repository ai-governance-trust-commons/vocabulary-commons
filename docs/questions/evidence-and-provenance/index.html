Research path 06 / 8 · Evidence and provenance

What can make apparently credible evidence unreliable?

Follow information from its origin into a later answer or plan. Ask whether its source remains visible and whether a bounded defense changes the result.

Keep the comparison bounded.

These studies use different tasks and attacks. They are not a head-to-head test, prevalence estimate or proof of universal defenses.

Selections express editorial relevance. Publication is not endorsement, independent verification or validation of a term.

Questions to carry into the reading

  1. Which upstream material influenced the output, and was it attributed?
  2. Did a monitor see the source material, a paraphrase, or only the final action?
  3. Which payloads, models and adaptive attacks were included in a defense test?

Reading 1 · EVID-095 · Reported observation

Follow an injected plan into a later trace

Why this is selected — Commons editorial note: Controlled tasks examine paraphrasing without attribution and monitor evasion, while preserving evidence that thinking monitors performed better on average.

Finding as recorded in the Library: Authors report that injected upstream plans can be paraphrased without attribution and evade reasoning-trace monitors in controlled coding and reasoning tasks.

Limits to retain: Two reasoning models and two scaling tasks; extreme-selected, single-monitor budget experiment does not estimate prevalence. Thinking monitors performed better on average. End-to-end delivery through RAG or multi-agent systems remains future work.

Reading 2 · EVID-183 · Reported observation

Compare a bounded source-marking defense

Why this is selected — Commons editorial note: Source-marking transformations reduced tested injection success, with synthetic payloads and specified older models limiting transfer.

Finding as recorded in the Library: Source-reported finding: Hines and colleagues report reduced prompt-injection success using input-source marking transformations.

Limits to retain: Synthetic corpus of 1,000 documents with a simple keyword payload and specified older GPT models; not proof of universal resilience against adaptive attacks or unchanged performance on all tasks.

What would strengthen the answer?

Look for comparable tasks and populations, independent source families, contrary results and directly public original sources. A useful contribution can be a correction, a missing connection or a reason the evidence does not fit.
