Research path 05 / 8 · Reliance and competence

How does organizational reliance affect the ability to evaluate work?

Compare assistance during work with the ability to evaluate or perform work after assistance ends. Retain evidence of learning and null results alongside concerns about lost practice.

Editorial path 0.5.6 · Public dataset 0.5.6 · Selected findings, not an exhaustive review.

Keep the comparison bounded.

School mathematics, clinical detection and workplace software differ in populations, tasks and measures. Reduced learning acquisition is not necessarily erosion of established skills. A statistically undetected difference is not proof of equivalence.

Selections express editorial relevance. Publication is not endorsement, independent verification or validation of a term.

Questions to carry into the reading

  1. What changed while assistance was available, and what persisted afterward?
  2. Was competence measured directly, or inferred from a performance proxy?
  3. Do the population, task and observation period support the proposed interpretation?

Reading 1 · EVID-185 · Reported observation

Separate assisted practice from unaided learning

Why this is selected — Commons editorial note: The classroom trial compares practice gains with later unassisted performance, while retaining the different Tutor result and the boundary between acquiring skills and losing established skills.

Finding as recorded in the Library: Source-reported finding: Bastani and colleagues report a classroom-randomized study of nearly 1,000 Turkish high-school students across four mathematics sessions. GPT Base and teacher-informed GPT Tutor increased assisted practice scores by 48% and 127% relative to control. Subsequent unassisted examination scores were 17% lower with Base; Tutor scores were statistically indistinguishable from control. Observation context: Fall semester of academic year 2023–2024.

Limits to retain: One school, grades 9–11, short exposure; assisted scores and immediate unassisted learning are different outcomes. Reduced acquisition does not establish erosion of established skills. Tutor required labor-intensive teacher inputs. Reported percentages are relative changes, not percentage points; no long-term outcome established. A statistically indistinguishable Tutor result does not establish equivalence or a positive unaided learning benefit.

Reading 2 · EVID-170 · Reported observation

Retain a study detecting no group-level skill change

Why this is selected — Commons editorial note: The clinical registry study supplies a bounded null result. No statistically detected change is not proof of equivalence.

Finding as recorded in the Library: Source-reported finding: A three-phase registry study before, during and after computer-aided detection found no statistically detected group-level post-exposure upskilling or deskilling. Inexperienced endoscopists improved during assistance; individual outcomes varied. Observation period and measurement context: November 2021–June 2025; three Norwegian clinics, 13 endoscopists, 5,013 procedures.

Limits to retain: PDR-5 endpoint rather than histological adenoma detection; nonrandomized phases, case mix, one device and small clinician sample. Null significance is not equivalence or proof against all skill loss. No raw data or replication inspected.

Reading 3 · EVID-175 · Reported observation

Examine suggestive evidence of learning

Why this is selected — Commons editorial note: Outage performance after workplace AI exposure offers a different possibility, with noisy estimates and limits on what chat duration measures.

Finding as recorded in the Library: Source-reported finding: The final journal study reports 5,172 support workers and a 15% average productivity gain. Workers exposed to AI retained faster chat handling during outages, which the authors interpret as suggestive learning. Some high-skilled workers showed small negative quality effects despite increasing adherence. Observation period and measurement context: Staggered rollout chiefly fall 2020/winter 2021; complete panel endpoints not established.

Limits to retain: Single firm, medium-run staggered observational adoption; outage chat duration does not measure individual-chat resolution. Outage estimates are noisy and may reflect chat mix. Does not establish durable generalized competence, long-run wages or aggregate labor demand. Data/code not independently analyzed.
