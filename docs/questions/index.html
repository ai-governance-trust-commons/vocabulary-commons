01 · Human oversight

When is human oversight more than an approval step?

Separate who makes a decision from who can recognize, assess and correct it—and whether there is time to act.

How to use a path

  1. Carry the comparison questions into the selected readings.
  2. Keep the recorded finding and its limitations together.
  3. Follow original citations and inspect the full Library record for interpretation, verification and explicit term relationships.
  4. Use coverage to identify curation gaps; broaden the Library search beyond the selected readings.

Source-family labels help identify overlap; they do not prove independence. A null result is not proof of no effect, and a conceptual argument is not a deployment measurement. These paths do not add evidence-to-term relationships.
