WHAT IS THE COMMONS?

An open reference site for AI governance: language, questions, discussion and research.

The Commons connects shared language and traceable evidence for examining oversight, organizational reliance, assurance, and governability in human–AI environments.

The founding principle
“Meaningful discussion requires sufficiently precise, shared language.”

Words are not evidence.

A term gives you a way to describe what you saw. A finding lets you trace what happened and where the evidence runs out. An author’s argument gives you an interpretation to question.

From the Evidence Library

Who gave the agent permission to move the money?

In a treasury demonstration, a Hyundai Card agent requested a currency conversion. A Kinexys agent checked the request against a signed mandate and booked a simulated transaction. No money moved.

What would you want to know next? If one institution withdrew that permission, how would the other know in time? The demonstration leaves that question open.
