What is the Commons?

An open place to make sense of AI.

The Commons is a public collection of terms and definitions for describing how AI is used and how it affects people and organizations. Anyone can explore the language, question a definition, or help improve it.

How to begin

Three ways to explore

The glossary starts with 35 terms that help describe AI capabilities, human oversight, organizational reliance, and emerging risks. You can look up a term, follow a question, or contribute a better explanation.

How to read an entry

Words are not evidence.

A useful term does not prove that every claim associated with it is true. Each entry shows whether its wording is established or still being developed, and how strongly its associated claims are supported.

Where this started

A published starting point.
An open future.

The first 35 definitions come from Describing the Environment We Are Trying to Govern by Lance J. Johnson. That version is preserved on Zenodo. The Commons makes it easier to explore and creates a place to question, refine, and add terminology without rewriting the original publication.
