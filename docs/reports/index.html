High-Signal Report Archive

Research reports reflecting assessments at the time, distinct from validated evidence findings. Archival inclusion is not independent verification. Evidence Library records retain their own provisional standing.

Dated research reports retain their sources, uncertainty and original assessments. Report dates differ from event and disclosure dates. Later corrections are appended visibly; the preserved report is not silently rewritten.
