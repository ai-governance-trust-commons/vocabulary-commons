Mechanism hypothesis

Vocabulary · EstablishedClaim · Foundation

Reading aid — founding standing: Conceptual or theoretical framing, rather than an empirical finding. Read the classification guide.

The v1.0.2 wording and classifications below are fixed. Evidence links use the current dataset; inspect each finding’s setting, source and limitations.

Definition

A provisional explanation specifying how certain conditions, activities, or relationships could produce an observed or anticipated effect. A useful hypothesis names evidence that could support, qualify, or weaken the explanation.

Relationship to other concepts

Investigative indicators can motivate the hypothesis; demonstrated behavior provides bounded observations against which it may be tested.
