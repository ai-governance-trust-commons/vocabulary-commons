Purposeful continuity

Vocabulary · WorkingNormative proposal

Definition

A proposed principle for evolving AI capabilities and successors: changes in methods, objectives, or architecture should preserve defensible connections to intended value and permit independent reconsideration. This remains a normative proposal, not a demonstrated alignment solution.

Relationship to other concepts

Purpose preservation concerns immediate objectives; successor assurance would examine whether specified properties persist as systems evolve.

## Purposeful continuity

**Vocabulary: Working**  
**Normative proposal**

A proposed principle for evolving AI capabilities and successors: changes in methods, objectives, or architecture should preserve defensible connections to intended value and permit independent reconsideration. This remains a normative proposal, not a demonstrated alignment solution.

**Relationship:** Purpose preservation concerns immediate objectives; successor assurance would examine whether specified properties persist as systems evolve.
