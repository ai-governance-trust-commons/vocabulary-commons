Successor assurance

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Foundation

Definition

Evaluation of whether a significantly modified or AI-created successor preserves specified behavioral, authorization, evidence, control, and purpose-related properties. Passing performance tests alone does not demonstrate preservation of these properties.

Relationship to other concepts

Purposeful continuity offers a normative rationale for preserving justified value; assurance independence is relevant to determining whether assessments can be trusted.
