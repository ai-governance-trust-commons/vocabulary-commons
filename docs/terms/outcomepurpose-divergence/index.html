Outcome–purpose divergence

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Demonstrated (bounded)

Reading aid — founding standing: Observed in specified settings; the label does not establish general prevalence or independent verification by the Commons. Read the classification guide.

The v1.0.2 wording and classifications below are fixed. Evidence links use the current dataset; inspect each finding’s setting, source and limitations.

Definition

A condition in which meeting an immediate objective or metric degrades the benefit or purpose that justified pursuing it. The divergence can occur through narrow measures or indirect consequences without an intent to deceive.

Relationship to other concepts

Purpose preservation and purposeful continuity are proposed principles meant to keep immediate success and continued development linked to underlying value.
