Founding glossary / v1.0.2

Assurance independence

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Foundation

Definition

An evaluator’s practical freedom to obtain necessary evidence, choose evaluation methods, form judgments, and report findings without undue control by the actor or system being evaluated. Separate organizational status alone is insufficient.

Relationship to other concepts

Chaperoning relies on independent supervisory judgment; reliable observability and reference-environment integrity help make independent assurance possible.
