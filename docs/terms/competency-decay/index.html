Competency decay

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Indicated

Reading aid — founding standing: Supported by evidence or inference, but not established generally; the scope remains open to investigation. Read the classification guide.

The v1.0.2 wording and classifications below are fixed. Evidence links use the current dataset; inspect each finding’s setting, source and limitations.

Definition

A decline in the human or organizational expertise needed to perform, verify, contest, or recover work that is repeatedly delegated to AI-enabled systems. It is especially consequential when skills needed for independent judgment are lost.

Relationship to other concepts

Organizational dependency can intensify the effects of competency decay, while chaperoning relies upon some retained evaluative competence.
