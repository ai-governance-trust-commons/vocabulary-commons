Horizon compression

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Indicated

Reading aid — founding standing: Supported by evidence or inference, but not established generally; the scope remains open to investigation. Read the classification guide.

The v1.0.2 wording and classifications below are fixed. Evidence links use the current dataset; inspect each finding’s setting, source and limitations.

Definition

A process in which the time available to identify, interpret, decide about, and intervene in consequential activity becomes shorter relative to the time intervention requires. It is an intervention-window problem, not a general label for every obstacle to correcting an agent.

Relationship to other concepts

BVR concerns recognition of relevant activities and relationships, not time. Feedback acceleration may shorten intervention windows, while trajectory persistence may impede behavioral correction despite an otherwise adequate window. Chaperoning requires observation, judgment, and effective action.

  • EVID-091 · The Foundation reports advancing the release by a day after premature disclosure, while organizational and compatibility constraints complicated remediation.
