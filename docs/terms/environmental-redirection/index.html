Environmental redirection

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Demonstrated (bounded)

Reading aid — founding standing: Observed in specified settings; the label does not establish general prevalence or independent verification by the Commons. Read the classification guide.

The v1.0.2 wording and classifications below are fixed. Evidence links use the current dataset; inspect each finding’s setting, source and limitations.

Definition

A change in an agent’s actions, methods, beliefs, or priorities arising from information, artifacts, tools, constraints, or incentives encountered while performing a task. Such redirection can be beneficial or harmful; an altered belief, selected method, and adopted objective are not the same outcome.

Relationship to other concepts

Reference-environment integrity concerns the quality of external inputs and records, while evidence laundering can alter apparent credibility. Trajectory persistence concerns continued pursuit of a selected course even after new evidence; it is not necessarily caused by external environmental manipulation.
