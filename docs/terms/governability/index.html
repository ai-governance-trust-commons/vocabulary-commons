Governability

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Foundation

Definition

A property of a consequential process describing the extent to which legitimate parties can understand, influence, constrain, correct, or recover its operation and outcomes. It does not imply complete predictability or a single controller.

Relationship to other concepts

Chaperoning is a form of active supervision within this larger property; system-level autonomy is a separate hypothesis concerning processes no participant can effectively direct.
