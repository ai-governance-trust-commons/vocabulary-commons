Observability boundary

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Foundation

Definition

The practical limit of what a given observer or monitoring arrangement can detect, interpret, or causally connect. The limit may be known, partly understood, or itself unrecognized.

Relationship to other concepts

BVR specifically calls attention to consequential relationships outside recognized relevance; the sleight-of-hand effect captures how valid local observation may be mistaken for sufficient overall coverage.
