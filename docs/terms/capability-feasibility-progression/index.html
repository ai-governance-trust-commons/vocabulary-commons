Capability feasibility progression

Vocabulary · ExtendedClaim · Foundation

Definition

An analytical sequence for describing distinct levels of practical possibility: possible → practical → repeatable → scalable. A step need not follow the preceding one automatically, and the categories are not a universal maturity model.

Relationship to other concepts

Constraint compression concerns the removal of barriers; this progression describes a potential result of that removal. Comparable staged capability reasoning appears in biosecurity studies [3].
