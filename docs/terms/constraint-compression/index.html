Constraint compression

Vocabulary · WorkingClaim · Demonstrated (bounded)

Definition

A reduction, often AI-enabled, in the practical barriers of knowledge, expertise, labor, cost, time, or coordination that once limited an activity. It describes the change in constraints, not the eventual impact of the activity.

Relationship to other concepts

Capability feasibility progression describes a potential resulting shift in what is practical, repeatable, or scalable. A similar mechanism is studied as capability uplift in biosecurity [3].
