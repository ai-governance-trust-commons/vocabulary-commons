Investigative indicator

Vocabulary · EstablishedClaim · Foundation

Definition

An observed characteristic or combination of signals that provides a reason to investigate a possible event, mechanism, or hidden relationship without establishing that it occurred.

Relationship to other concepts

It motivates testing a mechanism hypothesis and must not be presented as demonstrated behavior.
