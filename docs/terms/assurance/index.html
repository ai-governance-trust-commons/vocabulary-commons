Assurance

Vocabulary · EstablishedClaim · Foundation

Definition

Evidence-supported confidence that specified properties or outcomes will hold under stated objectives, operating conditions, and assumptions. Assurance has a defined scope and can lose relevance when those conditions change.

Relationship to other concepts

Attacker-model change can invalidate previously adequate evidence; assurance independence and reference-environment integrity affect the credibility of that evidence.
