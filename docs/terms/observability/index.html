Observability

Vocabulary · EstablishedClaim · Foundation

Definition

The capacity to obtain and interpret dependable evidence about states, actions, relationships, and consequences relevant to a specified question. Evidence may come from more than one system or organization.

Relationship to other concepts

An observability boundary describes the current limit of that capacity, while BVR describes relevant relationships beyond what the observer even recognizes as necessary to examine.
